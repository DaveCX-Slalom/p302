# Project Brief (summary)

**Product:** Interactive data story, "Why Do Patients Come Back?"
**Audience:** Dana, hospital COO / quality director, non-analyst.
**Point of view:** Overall readmissions look flat. Two conditions (heart failure, COPD) and weekend/under-staffed discharge shifts drive most of them. A small staffing change removes a large share.
**Takeaway for the user:** "It's two conditions and one shift pattern, and I can act on it."
**Structure:** 6 scroll beats: hook, break apart, where, why, what-if, takeaway.
**Constraints:** invented data, no client or Slalom info, Vue, password-protected deploy.
**Success:** passes Works / Repo / Design-and-thinking review. See `/BRIEF.md` for full detail.
